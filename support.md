# Juru — Support
  
  Welcome. This page covers the most common questions about using Juru. If you do not find an answer here, please email **<juru.support@gmail.com>** and we will
  respond within 48 hours.

  ## Requirements
  
  Juru runs on:
  - iPad with a **TrueDepth front camera** (iPad Pro 11" / 13" — 3rd generation or later, iPad Air 4th generation or later)
  - iPadOS 17 or later
  - Adequate ambient lighting (the front camera needs to see your face clearly)
  
  The app is **iPad only** in v1.0. iPhone support is on the roadmap.

  ## Frequently Asked Questions

  ### How does Juru work?
  Juru detects two facial gestures using the TrueDepth camera:
  - A **brow raise** to navigate between options.
  - A **lip pucker (kissing motion)** held for ~1.2 seconds to select, or ~2.7 seconds to undo.
  
  You compose text by navigating a binary tree of letter groups, then speak it aloud with a tap of a gesture.

  ### How do I recalibrate?
  Open the app's Settings (gear icon, top-right), then tap **Recalibrate**. The app will guide you through a fresh calibration to match your current
  facial range of motion.

  You should recalibrate if:
  - Your gestures feel too sensitive or not sensitive enough.
  - You are using the app in a noticeably different lighting condition.
  - Your physical condition has changed.

  ### Face tracking is not working — what should I do?
  1. Make sure your iPad has a TrueDepth camera (see Requirements above).
  2. Confirm Juru has camera permission in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera**.
  3. Move to a well-lit area. The front camera needs to see your face clearly.
  4. Hold the iPad in **portrait orientation** at eye level, ~30–60 cm from your face.
  5. Try recalibrating from the in-app Settings.
  
  ### Why is the app portrait only?
  Juru's gesture detection and interface are tuned for portrait orientation. Landscape support is on the roadmap for a future version.
  
  ### Does Juru work without internet?
  Yes — entirely. Juru runs 100% on your device and never makes any network requests. You can use it on a plane, off the grid, or in any environment
  without connectivity.
  
  ### Is my facial data stored or sent anywhere?
  No. The camera feed is processed in real time by Apple's on-device ARKit framework. **No video, image, or facial geometry is ever recorded or 
  transmitted.** Only numeric calibration values (e.g., your personal gesture thresholds) are saved, and they stay on your iPad.

  See our [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md) for details.
  
  ### Can I use Juru with VoiceOver, Dynamic Type, or Reduce Motion?
  Yes. Juru supports:
  - **VoiceOver** narration of all interactive elements.
  - **Dynamic Type** for larger text.
  - **Reduce Motion** — when enabled in iPad Settings, animations are simplified.
  
  ### How do I reset my onboarding?
  Open the app's Settings (gear icon, top-right), then tap **Reset Onboarding**. The next launch will start from the welcome flow.

  ### Is Juru a medical device?
  **No.** Juru is a communication assistance tool. It is not a medical device, and it does not diagnose, treat, or monitor any medical condition. If
  you have a clinical communication need, please consult a speech-language pathologist or assistive technology specialist.
