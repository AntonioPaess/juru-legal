# Privacy Policy

  **Effective Date:** June 4, 2026
  **Last Updated:** June 4, 2026
  
  Juru is built around a single principle: your data belongs to you. We collect nothing, store nothing on our servers, and have no servers to begin
  with.

  ## Summary

  - We do **not** collect any personal data.
  - We do **not** use analytics, tracking, or advertising.
  - We do **not** connect to the internet — Juru runs entirely offline.
  - We do **not** share any data with third parties.
  - All processing happens **on your device**.
  - Your calibration settings stay **on your iPad**, never leaving it.

  ## What Juru Accesses

  ### Camera
  Juru uses the front-facing TrueDepth camera to detect facial gestures (brow raises and lip puckers) in real time. The camera feed is processed
  locally on your device by Apple's ARKit framework. **No video, image, or facial geometry is recorded, stored, or transmitted.**
  
  You can revoke camera access at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera → Juru** on your iPad.
  
  ### Local Storage
  Juru saves a small amount of data locally on your device, never leaving it:
  - Your personal **calibration thresholds** (numeric values representing the strength of your facial gestures).
  - A flag indicating whether you have completed onboarding and the tutorial.

  This data is stored in the app's private container using `UserDefaults`. It cannot be accessed by other apps. It is removed if you uninstall Juru.

  ## What Juru Does Not Access

  - Your contacts, photos, location, microphone, or health data.
  - Any other apps or services on your device.
  - The internet — Juru has no networking capability and makes no requests to any server, ours or otherwise.
  
  ## Children's Privacy
  
  Juru does not knowingly collect any data from any user, including children. The app is rated 4+ and is safe for use by children. Because we collect
  nothing, there is no children's data to protect or delete.

  ## Your Rights

  Because we do not collect or process any personal data, traditional data subject rights (access, deletion, portability) do not apply — there is
  nothing for us to access, delete, or export. Any data Juru creates lives only on your device, and you control it directly through the app and iPad
  system settings.

  ## Changes to This Policy

  If we ever change how Juru handles data, we will update this policy and the **Last Updated** date above. Material changes will also be reflected in
  the App Store release notes for the affected version.
